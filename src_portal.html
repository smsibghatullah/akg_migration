About us

AKG hardware was established in 1991 and is a 100% Omani company specializing in providing the best equipment for moving, cutting and installation of tiles, and many other construction tools.

Our goal is to provide the latest international equipment to the Omani market to improve the quality and efficiency of the construction industry and to develop a culture of using the most advanced tools.
